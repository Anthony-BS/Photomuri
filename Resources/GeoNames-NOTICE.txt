Photomuri selects capitals and representative cities, extracts English/Korean
aliases, and adds a small set of island/region names and Korean aliases.
The bundled subset is modified from the source. It is used offline; no GeoNames
API account or network connection is needed when searching in Photomuri.
IANA time-zone rules and historical daylight saving offsets come from macOS.
GeoNames supplies the original data as-is without warranty of completeness.
